# The 3+3 Fuel Plan

*Iron Log · nutrition reference · v1*

Daily macro targets and a repeatable meal template to support the [3+3 Protocol](PROTOCOL.md) — VO₂max up, muscle held (or slightly gained), body fat drifting down slowly.

- **~2,700–2,800 kcal** — training days (6/week)
- **~2,450–2,550 kcal** — rest day & deload-week days
- **185–200 g protein** — every day, no exceptions
- **−0.25 to −0.5 lb/week** — the target scale trend

> Athlete basis: male · 32 · 6'2" · ~185 lb · ~16–18% BF. Mifflin-St Jeor BMR ≈ 1,860 kcal; with six ≤45-min sessions/week plus daily life, maintenance lands ≈ 2,900 kcal. The targets sit ~150–250 kcal under that weekly average — a *slow* cut that protects lifting performance and interval quality.

---

## Daily macro targets

| Day type | Calories | Protein | Carbs | Fat |
|----------|:--------:|:-------:|:-----:|:---:|
| Lift or hard-cardio day (Lower, Upper A/B, VO₂max, Threshold) | 2,700–2,800 | 185–200 g | 300–350 g | 65–80 g |
| Zone 2 / easy-Saturday day | 2,600–2,700 | 185–200 g | 280–320 g | 65–80 g |
| Rest day | 2,450–2,550 | 185–200 g | 250–280 g | 65–80 g |
| Deload / taper weeks (4 · 8 · 12) | use the rest-day tier most days | 185–200 g | — | — |

**How the levers work:** protein is fixed (~1 g/lb — it's what holds muscle in a deficit), fat is a floor (hormones), and **carbs are the dial** — they scale with the day's output and absorb any calorie adjustment. Add ~35–40 g fiber and front-load water on interval days.

## The daily template

Keep the two anchor meals — upgrade them slightly — and add two low-effort protein feedings. Training day lands ≈ 2,750 kcal · ~195 g protein.

| # | Meal | What | kcal | P | C | F |
|---|------|------|:----:|:--:|:--:|:--:|
| 1 | **Breakfast — the bowl, upgraded** | 111 g oats · 15 g honey · 1 cup berries · **40 g whey** (up from 32) · **1 tbsp peanut butter** stirred in | ~790 | 54 | 109 | 17 |
| 2 | **Lunch — the missing meal** | 40–50 g protein + a fist or two of carbs (options below) | ~600 | 45 | 55 | 20 |
| 3 | **Pre-workout** *(training days, 60–90 min out)* | 32 g whey shaken in 1 cup milk + a banana | ~345 | 33 | 42 | 5 |
| 4 | **Dinner — Katherine's plate, quantified** | 7–9 oz cooked protein · 1.5–2 cups rice or 350–400 g potato · big veg portion · ~1 tbsp olive oil/butter | ~950 | 60 | 80 | 30 |
| 5 | **Evening cap** | 170–225 g Greek yogurt (or cottage cheese) + berries | ~180 | 22 | 15 | 2 |
| | **Training-day total** | | **~2,770** | **~195** | **~300** | **~75** |

**Rest day:** drop meal 3 and trim dinner carbs by ~½ cup rice → ~2,500 kcal with protein untouched.

### Lunch rotation (pick one, zero cooking skill required)

- **Leftovers** — a palm-and-a-half of last night's protein + a cup of the carb
- **Tuna/chicken wrap** — 2 tortillas · 5 oz canned tuna or chicken · light mayo · greens
- **Deli plate** — 5–6 oz turkey · 2 slices sourdough · cheese · a piece of fruit
- **Yogurt bowl** — 250 g Greek yogurt · 60 g granola · banana · drizzle of honey (+ ½ scoop whey if handy)

### Timing vs the rotation

- The breakfast bowl is ideal fuel **2–3 h before a VO₂max 4×4 or threshold session**; if training earlier, the pre-workout shake + banana alone is enough.
- Hard-cardio days (VO₂max, Threshold Saturday) keep the **full carb allotment**; the easy-Saturday/Zone 2 days can run the middle tier.
- The evening yogurt doubles as slow pre-sleep protein — it's the cheapest muscle-retention insurance in the plan.

## Supplements (short list)

- **Creatine monohydrate — 5 g/day, any time.** The one clearly-supported add for holding muscle in a deficit.
- **Vitamin D** if not regularly in the sun; **fish oil** if fish is on the plate less than twice a week.
- Caffeine before the 4×4 is optional but earns its keep.

---

## Monitoring — the feedback loop

- Weigh daily, judge only the **weekly average**. Target −0.25 to −0.5 lb/week.
- Dropping faster → add ~150–200 kcal of carbs. Flat for 3+ weeks with no visible change → trim ~150 kcal (from carbs, never protein).
- Log meals against the running tally (the daily check-in tracks meal-by-meal macros and remaining targets).
- Performance is the tiebreaker: if interval pace/watts or bar speed sag while scale-weight falls, eat more — the deficit is winning the wrong contest.
